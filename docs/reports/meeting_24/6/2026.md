## How Supervisor Account Linking Works

The CSV file contains supervisor names as plain text — the system reads that name and tries to find a matching supervisor account. If the name matches exactly, it links automatically and the supervisor can log in to see their students. If the name is slightly different (due to titles or abbreviations), it appears in the Unlinked Supervisors panel where the coordinator can manually connect it to the right account.

## Supervisors Can See Their Students

Once a supervisor account is linked, they can log in and go to My Students to see all their assigned students with project titles and logbook activity. The supervisor overview panel also appears on the main dashboard automatically — showing assigned student count, who needs attention, and last logbook entry dates. Other roles (coordinator, student) do not see this panel.

## Supervisor Workload Distribution Chart

The analytics dashboard shows a bar chart of how many student pairs each supervisor is handling. This helps the coordinator quickly spot supervisors who are overloaded or underloaded. The chart updates automatically based on the imported CSV data.

## How Domain, Platform, and Industrial Type Will Be Determined

The current CSV does not include domain, platform, or project type information, so everything shows as "Others" or "Web App" for now. The next planned sprint will add rule-based classification — the system will read project titles and apply defined rules to categorize them automatically. After that, AI-assisted classification will be explored as an enhancement.

## Will Importing More CSVs Increase Total SE Students?

Yes — importing FYP 2 March 2026, FYP 1 October 2025, and FYP 2 October 2025 will add new student records and the total count will increase. Existing records are never deleted or overwritten — the system only adds new students it has not seen before. The FYP 1 and FYP 2 counts and the Previous semester numbers will update accordingly.

## Practical Cautions Before Importing CSV Files

Always set the correct phase (FYP 1 or FYP 2) and semester on screen before importing — the CSV does not carry this information and a wrong selection will mislabel the entire batch. After each import, check the skipped duplicates count to confirm no unexpected records were dropped. Back up the database before any import session by running: cp database/database.sqlite database/backup_YYYYMMDD.sqlite
